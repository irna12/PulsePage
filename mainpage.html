Welcome to PulsePage

PulsePage is a reading management website that helps users organize and monitor their reading activities efficiently. Users can add books, edit book details, and update their reading progress through an interactive dashboard. The website also includes a statistics section that categorizes books into total books, books that you are planning to read, current reading books, and finished books, allowing users to easily track their reading journey and progress.
